# 03 · Nest Rings

iOS app icon, 1024×1024 square, full-bleed, no rounded-rect baked in, no transparency, no text. Vertical Field Black gradient #2e343a to #0b0e11. Three concentric circular arcs sharing one center slightly below the canvas middle. Each arc sweeps 240 degrees and opens at the top (a nest, not Wi-Fi). Rounded caps. Inner arc brightest mint #b6f0d4, middle #7fd9b0, outer sage-mint at lower opacity. Soft glow on the inner arc only. Same geometry as the Home connect dot rings under the capsule. No left-right radio waves, no center bullseye disc, no radar sweep.
